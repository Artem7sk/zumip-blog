Best Booking Form Template for Dog Boarding Business: What to Include (and What to Skip)

If you run a boarding kennel, a grooming salon, or a mobile grooming van, you already know the truth: most of your admin headaches start with a bad booking form. A form that's missing the wrong field means a phone call at 9 PM. A form that asks for too much means the client abandons it halfway through.

This guide breaks down what actually belongs in the best booking form template for a dog boarding business, with concrete fields, examples, and a checklist you can copy today. No fluff, no software pitch — just the structure that works for teams of 1–10 people.

---

Why Your Booking Form Is a Business Tool, Not Just a Form

A booking form does four jobs at once:

1. Collects the info you need to provide safe care (vaccinations, feeding, behavior notes).

2. Sets expectations (drop-off/pick-up windows, cancellation policy, late fees).

3. Filters out problem bookings before they hit your calendar.

4. Creates a paper trail if a dispute happens later.

Most free templates online only do job #1. That's why kennels end up chasing clients for vaccination records or arguing about a no-show fee they never mentioned upfront.

---

The Core Fields Every Dog Boarding Booking Form Needs

Here's a practical field list. You can split it into a multi-step form (recommended for mobile) or keep it on one page if it's short.

Section 1: Owner & Emergency Contact

    • Full name
    • Phone (mobile preferred — you'll text)
    • Email
    • Secondary emergency contact (name, relationship, phone) — not optional
    • Vet name, clinic, and phone
    • Authorization to contact vet in an emergency (checkbox)

Section 2: Dog Profile

    • Dog's name, breed, age, weight
    • Spayed/neutered? (Yes / No / N/A)
    • Microchip number (optional but useful)
    • Vaccination status — Rabies, DHPP, Bordetella (upload or date)
    • Medications (name, dose, frequency)
    • Allergies or dietary restrictions
    • Feeding instructions (brand, amount, times)
    • Behavior notes: good with other dogs? Good with strangers? Resource guarding? Escape artist?

Section 3: Stay Details

    • Drop-off date and time window (not exact time)
    • Pick-up date and time window
    • Type of stay: boarding / daycare / grooming add-on
    • Preferred kennel size or suite (if you offer tiers)
    • Add-ons: bath before pickup, extra walk, photo updates

Section 4: Policies & Agreements

    • Cancellation policy acknowledgment
    • No-show / late pickup fee acknowledgment
    • Emergency vet authorization
    • Photo/media release (optional)
    • Signature + date

> Tip: If your form has more than 20 fields, split it into two steps: "Your info" and "Your dog's info." Completion rates drop sharply on long single-page forms.

---

What to Leave OUT of Your Booking Form

Less is more. Skip these unless they're truly required:

    • Payment card details — never collect these on a plain form. Use a payment link after confirmation.
    • Social Security numbers or driver's license numbers — not necessary for boarding.
    • Long essay questions ("Tell us about your dog's personality") — clients won't fill these out. Use a short dropdown instead.
    • Exact drop-off times — use windows. Exact times create bottlenecks and no-shows.

Booking Form Template Example (Copy-Ready Structure)

Here's a skeleton you can paste into Google Forms, Jotform, or a PDF:

```

OWNER INFO

    • Name: ______
    • Phone: ______
    • Email: ______
    • Emergency contact (name + phone): ______
    • Vet clinic + phone: ______

DOG INFO

    • Name / Breed / Age / Weight: ______
    • Vaccinations up to date? [ ] Yes [ ] No — upload proof
    • Medications: ______
    • Allergies: ______
    • Feeding instructions: ______
    • Behavior notes (check all that apply):
[ ] Good with other dogs

[ ] Anxious around strangers

[ ] Resource guards food/toys

[ ] Escape risk

[ ] Other: ______

STAY DETAILS

    • Drop-off date: ______ Time window: ______
    • Pick-up date: ______ Time window: ______
    • Service: [ ] Boarding [ ] Daycare [ ] Grooming add-on
    • Add-ons: [ ] Bath [ ] Extra walk [ ] Photo updates

POLICIES (must check to submit)

[ ] I agree to the cancellation policy

[ ] I agree to the late pickup / no-show fee

[ ] I authorize emergency vet care up to $____

Signature: ______ Date: ______

```

That's the backbone. Everything else is customization.

5 Common Booking Form Mistakes That Cost You Money

1. No cancellation or no-show policy on the form

If it's not on the form, it's not enforceable. Clients will argue. Put the fee in writing, with a checkbox.

2. No time windows

"Drop off at 10:00 AM" means five clients show up at 10:00 AM. Use "10:00 AM – 12:00 PM" instead.

3. Missing behavior questions

A dog with resource guarding issues in a shared play area is a liability. Ask upfront.

4. No vaccination proof field

Verbal confirmation isn't enough. Require an upload or a date + vet name.

5. No confirmation email template

The form is step one. What you send after matters just as much. A clear confirmation with drop-off instructions, what to bring, and your phone number cuts down on reply-all chaos.

How to Choose the Best Booking Form Template for Your Business

Use this quick decision checklist:

    • Do you board overnight? → You need emergency vet authorization + feeding instructions.
    • Do you offer grooming add-ons? → Add a grooming consent + matting disclaimer.
    • Do you have multiple staff? → Add an "assigned staff member" internal field (not client-facing).
    • Do you take deposits? → Add a deposit acknowledgment + payment link field.
    • Do you serve mobile clients? → Add an address + parking notes field.

If you check three or more of these, a generic free template won't cut it. You need one built for pet care specifically.

Quick Checklist: Is Your Booking Form Ready?

Before you publish it, run through this:

    • [ ] Emergency contact and vet info are required fields
    • [ ] Vaccination proof is requested (upload or date)
    • [ ] Behavior notes section exists
    • [ ] Drop-off and pick-up use time windows
    • [ ] Cancellation and no-show policies are visible and require a checkbox
    • [ ] Emergency vet authorization is signed
    • [ ] Form works on mobile (test it on your own phone)
    • [ ] Confirmation email template is written and ready
    • [ ] Reminder message template is written and ready

If any box is unchecked, fix it before your next busy season.
